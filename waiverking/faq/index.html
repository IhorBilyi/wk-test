FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what studio owners ask us most.

Do I need Mindbody to use WaiverKing?
Yes. WaiverKing works only with Mindbody and files every signed waiver to the client’s Mindbody profile. There is no standalone version.
How much does it cost?
From $15 a month for up to 30 signed forms. Your tier follows how many forms are filed in Mindbody each month, and the count resets monthly.
What counts as a form?
A form that a client signs, that is approved and filed in their Mindbody profile.
Do I have to build my forms?
No. Upload the waiver PDF you use today. AI turns it into a digital form and a person on our team checks it. Your first form is built free.
Can WaiverKing find existing clients and create new ones?
Yes. Each signer is matched to their Mindbody profile, and new clients get a profile created automatically.
Can parents sign for their kids?
Yes. One adult can add their children on the same form.
What happens during the 15-day trial?
You get the full product, no card needed. Test your link yourself before you share it with clients.
What happens after the trial?
We ask you to confirm, then bill your card monthly. You can cancel any time.
Where are signed waivers stored?
In Mindbody. They are sent in real time and not kept in WaiverKing after they are filed.
