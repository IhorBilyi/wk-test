What Is Online Waiver Software?

Online waiver software allows businesses to collect signed waivers, liability releases, consent forms, intake forms, questionnaires, and registration forms digitally instead of on paper.

Rather than printing documents, handing them to clients, scanning them later, and filing them manually, businesses can use a digital waiver system to collect information through an online form. Clients can complete forms from home, on their phone, on a tablet, at a front desk, or through a dedicated waiver kiosk.

A good waiver management system helps businesses:

  • Collect signed waivers online
  • Store completed waiver forms digitally
  • Reduce paper documents
  • Improve front desk efficiency
  • Reduce client registration mistakes
  • Keep client information more organized
  • Support electronic signatures
  • Manage intake forms, consent forms, and questionnaires
  • Make forms available before clients arrive
  • Improve the client onboarding experience

For businesses using MindBodyOnline, the real value comes when digital waiver software does more than simply collect a signed PDF. The real value is in connecting the waiver process to the client management system.
